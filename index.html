Help

How this pack pairs with SnowBid

SnowBid prices the work — cost per push, margin floor, season price. This pack papers it: the signed contract, the route sheet in the truck, and the storm log that proves what the season actually delivered. When a client's cap is in question, the Storm Log totals and the run history are your evidence; when renewal time comes, the same numbers feed next season's SnowBid pricing.

The documents

Season Contract — the full agreement: seasonal flat, per-push, or per-event service, trigger depth, visit cap and overage rate, salting, payment terms, and the liability language. Per-Push Agreement — the short form for residential per-push customers: price, trigger, salting, and signatures on one page. Route Sheet — print it, clip it to the visor, check stops off as you clear them. Client Terms Page — the explanations, written for clients, that prevent the three most common disputes (trigger, cap, salt).

⚠️ About the contract language — read this

The contract templates in this pack are sample planning language, not legal advice. Snow-removal liability — especially slip-and-fall responsibility — is governed by your state's law, your insurance policy, and how your contracts are actually written and signed. Laws and enforceable terms vary widely by state and by residential vs. commercial clients. Have a local attorney review and adapt this language before you use it with clients, and confirm with your insurance agent that your contract terms match what your policy expects. Do not rely on the sample slip-and-fall wording as-is.
